Koblenz's invisible lake

Two hours after sunset, cold air slides off the Hunsrück and the Westerwald and pools in the Rhine and Moselle valleys. The city sits at the bottom of a lake you cannot see.

How to read this map
  1. Blue haze is the modelled cold-air layer, 2 h after sunset on a clear, calm night. The deeper the blue, the thicker the layer.
  2. Moving streaks are the cold-air flow: direction and speed from the model, shown 300× faster than real time.
  3. On the plateaus the cold air forms and drains away, so they stay almost dry: Karthause, Ehrenbreitstein, Horchheimer Höhe.
